## Meeting Transcript 2: Business Requirements Session

**Meeting:** Business Requirements Gathering
**Date:** October 18, 2024
**Attendees:**
- AnyCompany: Mark Thompson (VP Operations), Jennifer Adams (VP Marketing), Robert Kim (VP Customer Experience)
- AnyTech: Rachel Green (Business Consultant), Jennifer Walsh (Partner Director)

**[Meeting Start - 2:00 PM]**

**Rachel Green:** Thank you for your time. We want to understand your business requirements and success criteria for this transformation.

**Mark Thompson:** From operations perspective, we need better visibility into our supply chain. Currently, it takes 4 hours to get accurate inventory data across all channels.

**Rachel Green:** What's the business impact of that delay?

**Mark Thompson:** We oversell products regularly, leading to customer disappointment and cancellations. We estimate significant annual revenue loss from inventory inaccuracies.

**Jennifer Adams:** From marketing standpoint, we need real-time customer insights. Our current analytics are batch-processed overnight, so we're always reacting to yesterday's data.

**Rachel Green:** Can you quantify the impact of delayed insights?

**Jennifer Adams:** We're missing personalization opportunities. Our conversion rates are 2.3%, but industry leaders achieve 4-5% with real-time personalization. That represents substantial additional revenue potential.

**Robert Kim:** Customer experience is my biggest concern. Our mobile app crashes during peak traffic, and page load times exceed 8 seconds. Our NPS score has dropped 15 points this year.

**Rachel Green:** What would success look like for customer experience?

**Robert Kim:** Sub-3-second page loads, 99.9% app availability, and NPS score above 50. We also need omnichannel consistency - customers should see the same inventory and pricing across web, mobile, and stores.

**Jennifer Walsh:** These requirements align perfectly with cloud capabilities. Real-time data processing, auto-scaling, and global content delivery are standard features.

**Mark Thompson:** What about integration complexity? We have systems from 15 different vendors.

**Rachel Green:** We'll implement an API-first architecture with a centralized integration platform. This actually simplifies vendor management and enables faster partner onboarding.

**Jennifer Adams:** Timeline is critical. We have major product launches planned for next year. Can the new platform support those?

**Jennifer Walsh:** Absolutely. Cloud platforms excel at rapid scaling for product launches. You'll have capabilities you don't have today.

**Robert Kim:** What about training and change management? Our teams are comfortable with current systems.

**Rachel Green:** We include comprehensive training and change management in our approach. Most clients see productivity improvements within 3 months of go-live.

**[Meeting End - 3:30 PM]**
